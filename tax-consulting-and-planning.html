Frequently Addressed Questions

Key considerations regarding our tax planning protocols, audit defense, and jurisdictional reach.

When is the ideal time to initiate formal corporate tax planning?

add
The optimal window is during Q2 or Q3 of the active operating year. Initiating planning before the fiscal year concludes allows our partners to execute structural transfers, capital expenditures, executive compensation allocations, and entity restructuring in real time. Year-end compliance merely records what occurred; proactive planning shapes what will occur.

How does KMH navigate multinational double-tax treaties and OECD Pillar Two?

add
Our international tax attorneys engineer transfer pricing and holding company frameworks that strictly comply with both bilateral Double Tax Avoidance Agreements (DTAAs) and evolving OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax standards. We substantiate substance, economic nexus, and arm's-length transfer pricing to insulate clients from retroactive withholding disputes.

Can KMH audit prior returns and claim missed retroactive R&D or state credits?

add
Yes. The IRS and most state departments of revenue permit amended returns within a standard 3-year statutory lookback window. We frequently perform forensic retroactive audits that recover hundreds of thousands in previously uncaptured Section 41 R&D credits, bonus depreciation deductions, and multi-state overpayments.

What happens if a position in the strategy blueprint triggers an IRS inquiry?

add
Every position we recommend is supported by a formal written memorandum complying with IRS Circular 230 regulations and established judicial precedent. In the event of an audit, our licensed partners step in as your designated legal representatives with Power of Attorney, managing all correspondences and meetings with zero additional billable fees for retained clients.

How does KMH coordinate with our existing internal CFO and bookkeeping staff?

add
We act as specialized external tax counsel rather than replacing your day-to-day controller or finance department. We supply your finance team with exact booking directives, journal instructions, and compliance schedules, allowing your internal personnel to focus on core operational metrics while we oversee statutory optimization.
